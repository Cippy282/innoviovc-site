Key service · Financial reports

Financial due diligence reports

Before an investment, acquisition or major financing you need a clear, independent view of the numbers. We prepare structured reports that investors, banks and funders can rely on.

WhatsApp Email

What we analyse

Quality of earnings

Normalised revenue and EBITDA, one-off items, customer concentration and margin sustainability.

Net debt and debt-like items

Financial debt, tax and social liabilities, guarantees, leases and off-balance-sheet commitments.

Working capital

Normal levels of inventory, receivables and payables; seasonality and financing needs.

Cash flow

Cash conversion, capital expenditure and debt service capacity.

Business plan & projections

Testing assumptions, scenarios and sensitivity analysis.

Risks & compliance

Tax risks, state aid, obligations from EU-funded projects (sustainability, financial corrections) and litigation with financial impact.

When you need it

  • Buy-side: before acquiring a company or a stake
  • Vendor due diligence: before selling your company or raising capital
  • For banks and lenders: investment loans and structured finance
  • For EU-funded projects: financial capacity, co-financing, SME status

How we work

Scope

Key questions and perimeter.

Data

Document request list and data room.

Analysis

Management interviews and financial analysis.

Report

Written report, summary and presentation.

What do I receive?

A written report with an executive summary, adjustments, identified risks and recommendations, plus a presentation for decision-makers.

How long does it take?

Typically two to six weeks from receipt of documents, depending on complexity.

Does it replace a statutory audit?

No. Due diligence is decision-oriented (investment, acquisition, financing) and complements the financial audit rather than replacing it.
