About

FAQ

Questions about EU funding in Romania

What EU funding consulting does INNOVIO VC provide?

We cover the full life cycle of a funded project: finding the right call, eligibility review, funding strategy, application and business plan, submission, assessment clarifications, procurement, implementation, reporting and payment claims.

Do you work with foreign companies investing in Romania?

Yes. We help international investors understand which Romanian and EU programmes and state aid schemes apply to their investment, and we manage the application and compliance process locally.

Which funding programmes are open in Romania now?

For 2021–2027 these include the Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), POCIDIF, eight Regional Programmes, the Health Programme, the Education and Employment Programme and the Just Transition Programme, plus EU-managed programmes such as Horizon Europe and Digital Europe. Our open-calls page is updated daily from the official source.

Can you handle procurement inside a funded project?

Yes. We prepare tender documents and specifications under Law 98/2016, run private-beneficiary procedures and evaluate bids so that costs remain eligible at audit.

How quickly can I find out if my project is eligible?

Send us your sector, region, type of investment and estimated value on WhatsApp or by email, and we will reply with the relevant programmes and next steps.

Why does state aid matter?

Most business grants are granted as state aid under the General Block Exemption Regulation (EU) 651/2014 or as de minimis aid. The classification determines aid intensity, eligible costs and your obligations after the project ends.
