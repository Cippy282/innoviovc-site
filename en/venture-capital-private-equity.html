What we do

Funding strategy

How much capital you need, in which stages and from which sources: equity, debt, financial instruments, EU grants or a mix.

Investor readiness

Pitch deck, 3–5 year financial model, indicative valuation, use of funds and the KPIs investors track.

Investor targeting

Venture capital and private equity funds, strategic investors and family offices matched to your sector, stage and ticket size, in Romania and internationally.

Data room & due diligence

We organise company documents and prepare answers for investor checks.

Term sheet & negotiation

We help you assess offers, governance terms and exit provisions, together with your lawyers.

Blended finance

Combining private capital with EU grants, financial instruments (EIF, EBRD, funds of funds) and state aid to limit dilution.

How large does my company need to be?

We work with start-ups raising a first round and with mature companies seeking private equity. Growth potential and the team matter most.

Can I combine investment with EU funds?

Yes. Blended finance is one of our specialities: EU grants and financial instruments can reduce the equity you need and therefore dilution.

Do you guarantee the investment?

No. Investors decide. We improve your chances through rigorous preparation, the right investor list and well-structured negotiation.
